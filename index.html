Privacy Policy — Geo Nabaa

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Geo Nabaa ("the app", "we", "us") is a community application for geologists, geology students, and earth-science enthusiasts in Iraq and the wider Arabic-speaking world. This policy explains what information the app collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

By creating an account or using Geo Nabaa, you agree to this policy.

Developer: Ali Hashim
Contact email: amxdillw@gmail.com
Application: Geo Nabaa (Android package and iOS bundle ID com.nabba.geoapp)

1. Information we collect

1.1 Information you give us when you create an account

  • Phone number — used as your account identifier and how you sign in. We do not send SMS messages and we do not use your phone number for marketing.
  • Password — stored only as a cryptographic hash by our authentication provider. We never store or see your password in readable form.
  • Security question and answer — you choose a security question at sign-up so you can recover your account without SMS or email. Your answer is stored only as a SHA-256 hash and is never readable by us or by the app.
  • Display name — the name shown next to your content.
  • Country — the country you live in, selected from a list (Iraq is preselected). This is information you choose yourself. It is not device location, and the app never requests GPS or location permission.
  • Governorate — if your country is Iraq, the Iraqi governorate you select from a list. Like your country, you choose it yourself; it is not device location.
  • Profile picture — optional, uploaded by you.

1.2 Content you create in the app

Questions, answers, articles, comments, votes, likes, bookmarks, uploaded images, and content reports. Content you post publicly (questions, answers, articles, and the display name and profile picture attached to them) is visible to other users of the app, including users who are not signed in.

If you block another member, we store that block so their content stays hidden from you. Only you can see your block list, and it is erased when either account is deleted.

1.3 Photos and camera

The app asks for access to your photo library or camera only when you choose to attach an image — a profile picture or an image inside an article or question. We access only the specific images you pick. We do not scan, browse, or upload your photo library.

1.4 Device and technical information

  • Push notification token — a token issued by Firebase Cloud Messaging so we can deliver notifications to your device (for example, when someone answers your question). It is deleted when you delete your account.
  • Locally stored data — your session and a cache of recently viewed content are stored on your own device so the app works on a slow or interrupted connection. This data stays on your device and is cleared when you log out or uninstall the app.

1.5 What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your device location, GPS coordinates, or precise location of any kind.
  • We do not collect your contacts, call logs, SMS messages, or calendar.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers and we do not show ads.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell your personal information to anyone, and we never have.
  • We do not collect payment information — the app is free and contains no purchases.

2. How we use your information

We use the information above only to:

  • create your account, sign you in, and keep your session active;
  • let you recover your account through the security question if you forget your password;
  • display your content, display name, and profile picture alongside what you post;
  • send you notifications about activity related to you, such as replies to your questions;
  • maintain your reputation score and profile;
  • review reported content, enforce our community rules, and suspend accounts that break them;
  • keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems in the app.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making.

3. How your information is shared

We share your information only with the service providers required to run the app:

We may disclose information if we are legally required to do so, or where it is necessary to protect the safety of our users or the integrity of the service.

Administrators. Our moderators use a separate internal application connected to the same database. They can view reported content, approve or remove content, and suspend accounts. They cannot read your password or your security answer, because both are stored only as hashes.

4. Data retention and deleting your account

Your account data is kept for as long as your account exists.

You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Settings → Delete account inside the app. You may also request deletion by emailing amxdillw@gmail.com from the account you wish to delete.

When you delete your account:

  • Permanently erased: your login credentials and password hash, your profile, your security question and its hashed answer, your bookmarks, your likes, your notifications, your push notification tokens, and every profile picture you uploaded.
  • Kept but fully anonymized: questions, answers, articles, comments, votes, and reports you created are detached from you — the link to your identity is removed, so the content remains for the community's benefit with nothing that points back to you. It is no longer personal data and cannot be traced to your account.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Backups are purged on our provider's ordinary backup cycle, within 30 days.

5. Security

  • Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider and are never stored in readable form.
  • Security answers are stored only as SHA-256 hashes, in a separate table that the app has no permission to read.
  • All communication between the app and our servers uses encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections.
  • Access to data is enforced at the database level with row-level security policies, so users can only read and write what they are entitled to.

No system is perfectly secure, but we take these measures seriously and work to protect your information.

6. Children's privacy

Geo Nabaa is intended for users aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child under 16 has created an account, please contact us at amxdillw@gmail.com and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

You may at any time:

  • access the personal information in your profile, from inside the app;
  • correct your display name, country, governorate, and profile picture from Profile → Edit profile;
  • change your phone number and password from the app's settings;
  • delete your account and personal data, as described in section 4;
  • turn off notifications from your device's system settings.

For any request about your data, contact amxdillw@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

8. International users

The app is operated from Iraq and our service providers store data on servers that may be located in other countries. By using the app you consent to your information being processed in those locations, under the safeguards described in this policy.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page, and we will notify you in the app if the changes are significant. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
